# AZ-104 Day 01 — Summary Report (Roman Urdu)

## Overview
Instructor ne Azure Administrator ka roadmap bataya aur **15 physical servers** ke data-center scenario se requirement gathering, facility planning, CAPEX/OPEX aur SLA samjhaya.

## Learning objectives
- AZ-900, AZ-104 aur AZ-305 ke general pathway ko samajhna.
- Identity, storage, compute, network aur monitoring ke areas pehchanna.
- Traditional IT, virtualization aur cloud ka difference samajhna.
- Customer se 15 servers ke liye sahi sawalat karna.
- CAPEX/OPEX aur Response/Resolution SLA ko alag karna.

## Topics
Introductions; course roadmap; AVD; networking fundamentals; data-center roles; private/shared facilities; cooling, power, rack, remote access; P1–P5 priority.

## Technical concepts
Business service samjhe baghair hardware sizing na karein. Rack aur power failures ka risk assess karein. Purchase cost ke saath recurring expenses bhi dekhein. SLA company-specific agreement hota hai.

## Practical scenario
Ek customer shared data center mein 15 servers lagwana chahta hai. Students ne RAM, CPU aur storage poocha. Instructor ne poocha: location kya hai? Cooling kaisi hai? PDU fail ho to kya hoga? Physical entry kaise milegi? Customer ki actual services kya hain aur 15 ka number kyun?

## Key takeaways
1. Customer aur provider dono ki perspective se sochna seekhein.
2. Hardware se pehle requirements clear karein.
3. Redundancy aur security ko architecture ka hissa banayein.
4. CAPEX aur OPEX mein farq samjhein.
5. Ticket acknowledge karna aur issue restore karna alag SLAs hain.

## Interview revision
15 servers ki sizing kaise verify karoge? Colocation kya hai? PDU kya hai? P1 aur P5 mein kya difference hai? Azure administrator kin areas ko manage karta hai?

## Suggested labs (extra suggestions)
Requirements checklist likhein, power/rack diagram banayein, cost model karein, P1 incident escalation template banayein aur Azure portal ka safe exploration karein.
