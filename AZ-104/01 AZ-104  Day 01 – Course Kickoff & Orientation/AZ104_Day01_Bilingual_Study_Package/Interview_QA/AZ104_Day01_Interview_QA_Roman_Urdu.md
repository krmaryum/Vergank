# AZ-104 Day 01 — Interview Q&A (Roman Urdu)

> Questions are derived from lecture themes. Answers include additional technical clarification and are not direct instructor quotes.

## Q1. What is AZ-104 primarily about?

**Answer:** AZ-104 Azure administrator responsibilities cover karta hai: identity/governance, storage, compute, networking aur monitoring.

## Q2. How does AZ-900 differ from AZ-104?

**Answer:** AZ-900 basic concepts hai; AZ-104 mein practical Azure administration aur decisions par focus hota hai.

## Q3. What is the purpose of AZ-305?

**Answer:** AZ-305 Azure infrastructure solutions ki design/architecture par focused hai.

## Q4. Why should a cloud admin understand on-premises data centers?

**Answer:** On-prem ke networking, availability, failure aur capacity concepts cloud design mein bhi kaam aate hain.

## Q5. What is a business workload?

**Answer:** Business workload applications, services, data aur dependencies ka combination hota hai jo business ka kaam karta hai.

## Q6. What should you ask before sizing 15 servers?

**Answer:** Pehle services, usage, peak load, growth, redundancy, compliance aur ownership pooch kar 15 ka number verify karein.

## Q7. How do you estimate CPU and RAM?

**Answer:** Application metrics, peak users, target performance, headroom aur vendor recommendations se CPU/RAM size karein.

## Q8. What is a colocation facility?

**Answer:** Colocation provider ki facility hai jahan customer apna hardware rakh kar space, power aur connectivity leta hai.

## Q9. Is colocation the same as public cloud?

**Answer:** Nahin. Colocation mein usually customer ka hardware hota hai; public cloud mein on-demand abstracted services hoti hain.

## Q10. What is a private data center?

**Answer:** Private data center ek organization ke apne infrastructure ke liye hota hai.

## Q11. Why check geographic location?

**Answer:** Location se hazards, latency, legal rules, access aur DR planning affect hoti hai.

## Q12. Why does cooling matter?

**Answer:** Cooling, airflow aur humidity hardware reliability ke liye important hain.

## Q13. What is a rack PDU?

**Answer:** Rack PDU rack ke devices ko electricity distribute karta hai.

## Q14. How do dual PSUs improve availability?

**Answer:** Dual PSU independent power feeds par hon to ek PSU ya feed failure ka impact kam ho sakta hai.

## Q15. Why spread servers across racks?

**Answer:** Multiple racks mein distribute karne se single-rack failure ka risk kam hota hai.

## Q16. What is remote-hands support?

**Answer:** Remote-hands mein data-center staff customer ki taraf se approved physical work karte hain.

## Q17. Who may enter a shared data center?

**Answer:** Sirf authorized log provider ke access aur escort rules follow karke enter karte hain.

## Q18. What is CAPEX?

**Answer:** CAPEX long-lived assets jaise servers khareedne ya improve karne ka capital kharcha hai.

## Q19. What is OPEX?

**Answer:** OPEX services chalane ka recurring kharcha hai: bijli, staff, support aur subscriptions.

## Q20. Is every expense after startup OPEX?

**Answer:** Nahin. Baad mein naya server purchase karna bhi CAPEX ho sakta hai.

## Q21. What is an SLA?

**Answer:** SLA service targets, measurement aur responsibilities ka documented agreement hai.

## Q22. How is response SLA different from resolution SLA?

**Answer:** Response acknowledgement time hai; resolution service fix/restore karne ka time hai.

## Q23. What do P1–P5 mean?

**Answer:** P1–P5 priority labels hain; exact meaning aur timings organization ke SLA par depend karte hain.

## Q24. How would you handle a P1 incident?

**Answer:** P1 ko acknowledge, impact assess, team alert, mitigate, escalate, document aur restore karein.

## Q25. Should high CPU alone always be P1?

**Answer:** Nahin. Sirf high CPU P1 nahin; actual business impact dekhna hota hai.

## Q26. Why ask customers about ownership of servers?

**Answer:** Ownership se procurement, license, maintenance, access aur support responsibilities change hoti hain.

## Q27. What is a failure domain?

**Answer:** Failure domain woh components hain jo ek failure se saath impact ho sakte hain, jaise ek rack.

## Q28. What is a virtual network?

**Answer:** Virtual network cloud software mein defined logical network hai jo workloads ko connect karta hai.

## Q29. What is Azure Virtual Desktop?

**Answer:** Azure Virtual Desktop Azure par remote desktops aur applications deliver karne ki service hai.

## Q30. What metrics should an administrator monitor?

**Answer:** CPU, RAM, disk, latency, failures, resource health aur costs monitor karein.
