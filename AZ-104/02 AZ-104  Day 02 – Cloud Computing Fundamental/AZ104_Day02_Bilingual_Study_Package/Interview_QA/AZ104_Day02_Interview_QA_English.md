# AZ-104 Day 02 — Interview Questions & Answers (English)

> These are supplementary interview-preparation answers based on lecture topics; they are not a verbatim classroom exchange.

## Q1. What is on-premises infrastructure?

**Answer:** Company-managed physical computing, networking, storage and facilities deployed at its own or contracted premises.

## Q2. What does DHCP provide?

**Answer:** It dynamically leases IP configuration, including address and often DNS/gateway options, to client devices.

## Q3. What is DNS?

**Answer:** A distributed naming system that resolves names to records such as IP addresses.

## Q4. Why use AD DS?

**Answer:** It centrally manages enterprise identities, authentication and policy for domain-joined systems.

## Q5. Explain CAPEX versus OPEX.

**Answer:** CAPEX is capital investment in long-lived assets; OPEX covers recurring operating costs.

## Q6. What is disaster recovery?

**Answer:** A planned capability to restore critical services after a major disruption, often at an alternate location.

## Q7. Define RTO.

**Answer:** Recovery Time Objective: target maximum time to restore service after a disruption.

## Q8. Define RPO.

**Answer:** Recovery Point Objective: maximum tolerable data loss measured in time.

## Q9. Why use virtualization?

**Answer:** Run isolated workloads on shared hosts, improving utilization, manageability and provisioning flexibility.

## Q10. What differs between Type 1 and Type 2 hypervisors?

**Answer:** Type 1 runs directly on host hardware; Type 2 runs over a general-purpose host OS.

## Q11. Can every VM gain RAM or vCPU with zero downtime?

**Answer:** No. Hot-add depends on hypervisor support, VM configuration, guest OS and workload.

## Q12. What is public cloud?

**Answer:** Provider-operated, generally multi-tenant cloud services offered to customers.

## Q13. What is private cloud?

**Answer:** Cloud infrastructure dedicated to a single organization, on-premises or hosted.

## Q14. What is hybrid cloud?

**Answer:** An integrated use of private/on-prem and public-cloud environments with portability or connectivity.

## Q15. What is multi-cloud?

**Answer:** Using services from two or more cloud providers.

## Q16. What is pay-as-you-go?

**Answer:** Billing based on measured resource consumption, subject to individual service pricing rules.

## Q17. What is vertical scaling?

**Answer:** Increasing resources such as CPU or RAM allocated to a single instance.

## Q18. What is horizontal scaling?

**Answer:** Adding more instances of a service to distribute load.

## Q19. What is high availability?

**Answer:** Architecture that reduces service interruptions through redundancy and resilient operation.

## Q20. What does IaaS provide?

**Answer:** Cloud infrastructure resources; customers generally manage guest OS, applications and data.

## Q21. What does PaaS provide?

**Answer:** Managed platform/runtime so customers focus on building and operating their applications.

## Q22. What does SaaS provide?

**Answer:** Provider-hosted ready-to-use software accessed by customers.

## Q23. Does stopping an Azure VM always stop all charges?

**Answer:** No. Attached disks, reservations, IP/network resources or other billable components can remain.

## Q24. How do you choose an Azure region?

**Answer:** Consider latency, data residency, available services, resilience, budget and compliance.

## Q25. What is a cloud shared responsibility model?

**Answer:** Security and operational responsibilities are divided between provider and customer based on service type.

## Q26. How would you protect the diamond-business site?

**Answer:** Use identity controls, MFA, least privilege, backups, monitoring, secure networking and tested recovery procedures.

## Q27. How can cloud costs be controlled in a lab?

**Answer:** Budgets, cost alerts, auto-shutdown, tags and deleting unused resources while checking residual charges.

## Q28. What does cloud not eliminate?

**Answer:** Need for sound architecture, data governance, security, monitoring and disaster recovery.

## Q29. What is the airline analogy in the lecture?

**Answer:** Purchasing a ticket rather than an entire plane illustrates consuming shared infrastructure instead of owning it.
